# Experience CabinSafe — Recruiter Walkthrough (≈ 5 minutes)

Open the link on your phone. For the best experience, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iPhone) or **Install app** (Android), then launch it from the icon.

**Opening (about 10 seconds):** a private jet crosses the screen as *CabinSafe* fades in and out. Next comes the dedication, *Custom App created by Kate Keaney for Kevin Carnes at Aviation Search Group*, and then Kate's introduction and three pillars. Tap **Take the 5-minute tour** to start the guided demonstration, or **Explore on my own**. *Skip* is available throughout, and **More → Replay Intro** plays it again.

Otherwise, on the home screen, tap **Experience CabinSafe** and keep "Start from the original demo state" selected. A guide card at the bottom tracks your progress (Step x of 6). You can collapse it, go back, skip a step or exit at any time.

**Scenario:** fictional flight **CSI101, Miami (KMIA) → Geneva (LSGG)**, departing tomorrow aboard a privately owned 2026 Gulfstream G700 (conceptual configuration). On board: the principal (alias Principal), family, a frequent guest, the family Vizsla and two senior family members with documented accommodations.

| Step | What to do | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Flight Dashboard** | Look at the readiness ring and the separate *task completion* bar. Note the red **Not Ready** blocker: the AED's daily inspection is due. | Readiness ≠ checklist percentage. A critical blocker prevents a "Cabin Prepared" status, and departure authority always rests with the Pilot in Command. |
| **2 · Risk Advisory** | Open the **SIMULATED** Bise wind advisory for Geneva. Read *Why it matters to cabin preparation*, then tap **Acknowledge** with "Create a cabin preparation task" ticked. | Advisories always show category, severity, source, publication and retrieval times. Simulated data is never passed off as live, and the live-source panel shows each feed's real status. |
| **3 · Passenger Safety** | On **Rebecca**'s profile, review the documented mobility accommodation and the rule-generated tasks (boarding assistance, seat review, and a *confirmation* task for medical equipment). | Preparation comes only from documented records, never from assumed treatment. Restricted medical detail is visible to the Lead CA only. |
| **4 · Inspect the Aircraft** | On the cabin map, the pulsing amber **AED-01** opens. Tap **Inspect → Mark all as pass → Record inspection**. | The inspection updates the equipment record, completes the linked checklist item, clears the blocker and shows the readiness change (before → after). |
| **5 · AI Briefing** | Tap **Generate Flight Safety Briefing**. Scroll the ten sections; try **Copy** or **Export**. Optionally ask: *"What is the evacuation procedure?"* | The briefing is built from live records with document citations. The Copilot refuses to invent aircraft procedures: "Approved aircraft-specific documentation is required…" |
| **6 · Crew Handover** | As Lead CA, assign an open item to Sam Whitaker. Tap **Switch to Sam's demo view**, tick each acknowledgement and **Accept handover**. | Accountability: the task moves to Sam's queue, the acceptance is time-stamped, and the audit trail records every step. |

**Bonus — Pre-Boarding (Boarding tab):** switch to Sam Whitaker with Demo Role Preview and open **Boarding**. Tick the eight **everyday checks**, then the three items **added by others** (from Kate, Captain Hart and the Principal's PA), then tap **Confirm Pre-Boarding Complete**. If a critical blocker remains (for example, the AED inspection), sign-off stays locked until it is resolved. As the PA or a pilot, tap **Add item** to put something on the cabin attendant's checklist.

Finish on **Audit history** to see every action you took, each with actor, role and timestamp.

### Good to know
- **Demo Role Preview** (top-right chip) switches perspective among Lead CA, contract attendants, pilots, security and the personal assistant. It is a preview only, not authentication.
- **More → Reset Demo** restores the original scenario at any time.
- Everything is fictional and synthetic. Aircraft-specific content is labelled *Demonstration — Aircraft Verification Required*.
- Live AI runs only if the host configured a key. Otherwise the Copilot is clearly labelled as a deterministic demonstration assistant.
